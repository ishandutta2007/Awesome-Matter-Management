# Awesome-Matter-Management

## Top Matter Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Legal Matters, Case Tracking, Spend Management, Intake, eBilling & In-House / Law Firm Operations*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Matter Management**. These systems help legal departments and law firms track matters/cases, manage intake, collaborate on work, control outside-counsel spend, handle eBilling, and report on legal operations.



**Examples** include SimpleLegal, Legal Tracker (Thomson Reuters), Mitratech TeamConnect, HighQ, Onit, Checkbox, LawVu, BusyLamp, CaseFox, Actionstep, Brightflag, Docketwise, and CARET Legal (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise matter management and legal-ops platforms are predominantly commercial. Practical open options include **self-hosted case/practice management systems** (LawLink, OpenLawOffice), emerging open legal workspaces (Stella), and general case/CRM tools adapted for legal work. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about enterprise feature gaps.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[SimpleLegal](https://www.simplelegal.com/)**  

  Legal operations platform popular with mid-market in-house teams for matter management, spend tracking, and legal ops workflows.



- **[Legal Tracker (Thomson Reuters)](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/)**  

  Enterprise matter and spend management solution with strong analytics, eBilling, and integration into the Thomson Reuters legal ecosystem.



- **[Mitratech TeamConnect](https://mitratech.com/)**  

  Highly configurable enterprise legal management platform for matters, spend, documents, workflows, and compliance.



- **[HighQ (Thomson Reuters)](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/)**  

  Legal collaboration and workspace platform used for matter-centric work, client portals, and secure collaboration.



- **[Onit](https://www.onit.com/)**  

  Enterprise legal operations platform supporting matter management, workflow automation, and business processes for legal departments.



- **[Checkbox](https://www.checkbox.ai/)**  

  No-code legal workflow and automation platform often used for intake, matter routing, and process automation.



- **[LawVu](https://lawvu.com/)**  

  Modern legal workspace for in-house teams unifying matters, intake, contracts, spend, and collaboration.



- **[BusyLamp](https://www.busylamp.com/)**  

  Legal spend and matter management solution focused on eBilling, invoice review, and outside-counsel management.



- **[CaseFox](https://www.casefox.com/)**  

  Practice and matter management software used by law firms for case tracking, time, billing, and client management.



- **[Actionstep](https://www.actionstep.com/)**  

  Practice management platform for law firms covering matters, workflows, accounting, and client work.



- **[Brightflag](https://www.brightflag.com/)**  

  AI-assisted legal spend and matter management platform with strong invoice review and analytics capabilities.



- **[Docketwise](https://www.docketwise.com/)**  

  Immigration-focused practice and matter management software for forms, cases, and client workflows.



- **[CARET Legal](https://caretlelegal.com/)**  

  Legal practice management platform supporting matters, billing, and firm operations.



- **[Additional TeamConnect / Legal Tracker ecosystem offerings](https://mitratech.com/)**  

  Expanded enterprise legal management and spend capabilities under Mitratech and Thomson Reuters portfolios.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[LawLink](https://github.com/lawflow-boop/LawLink)**  

  Open-source, self-hosted case and practice management system for lawyers and small firms—covering intake, matters, tasks, deadlines, documents, and basic financials.



- **[OpenLawOffice](https://github.com/NodineLegal/OpenLawOffice)**  

  Open-source law office management project including case management, billing, tasking, and related practice features.



- **[Stella (open-source legal workspace)](https://stll.app/)**  

  Emerging open-source legal workspace focused on matters, documents, review, and AI-assisted work with self-hosting options.



- **[General open case / matter trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Community case-management and project-tracking tools adapted by legal teams for matter pipelines and deadlines.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo legal and project modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open ERP modules that can be configured for matter-like project tracking, time, and basic billing.



- **[Document and knowledge open repositories for matters](https://github.com/)**  

  Open document-management and wiki systems used to organize matter files and know-how under firm control.



- **[Time-tracking and billing open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open timekeepers and invoicing components sometimes combined with matter records for small practices.



- **[Workflow and intake open engines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open workflow tools used to build legal intake forms, approvals, and routing outside a full ELM suite.



- **[eBilling and invoice open parsers (experimental)](https://github.com/)**  

  Limited open utilities for parsing legal invoices—rarely sufficient alone for enterprise LEDES/eBilling compliance.



- **[Conflict-check and party open databases](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open approaches to maintaining party and conflict lists for smaller firms.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **LawLink** or similar self-hosted systems for small firms that want full data ownership.

- Using **Stella** or open workspaces when document-centric matter collaboration is the priority.

- Adapting open CRM/project tools for lightweight matter tracking when budget is constrained.

- Accepting that enterprise matter management, sophisticated eBilling, outside-counsel guidelines, and legal-ops analytics still favor commercial platforms (TeamConnect, Legal Tracker, LawVu, SimpleLegal, Brightflag, Onit, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control for small practices, and avoiding lock-in for internal workflows.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture intake in open forms or ticketing → track matters in LawLink or an open workspace → store documents under controlled repositories → handle time/billing carefully → escalate complex spend and eBilling to a commercial ELM platform when needed. Suitable for solo and small firms with technical support. Most in-house legal departments and larger firms continue to adopt commercial matter management platforms for compliance, spend control, and reporting.
